Who are we?

We are a family owned resort chain whos main goal is to provide a tropical oasis for families who desire to connect with nature and relax with the whole family.

Our History

It all started with an idea. In our culture which is of Hispanic decent we have a saying "El que no arriesga no gana" Meaning if you dont risk anything,you gain nothing. Pedro Pascal risked everything in 2004 to build up what is now one of the most famous resort chains in the U.S. The Pascal family has run the Pacific Trails resorts for over 20 years. We are extremely proud of the success and grateful to Grandfather Pedro for all his hard work.

Mission Statement

Leave the stress at home. At Pacific Trails we ensure fun and adventure for the entire family. We provide safe and secure nature based adventure without the use of technology! Disconnect and let the relaxation begin at Pacific Trails.
