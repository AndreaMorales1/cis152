The Yurts at Pacfic Trails

What is a Yurt?
our luxury yurts are permanent strucutres four feet off the ground.Each yurt is fully enclosed with canvas walls,a wodden floor and a roof dome that can be opened.
How are the Yurts finished?
Each yurt is furnished with a queen-size bed with down quilt and gas-fired stove. Your luxuary camping experinece included electricity and a sink with hot and cold running water. Shower and restroom facilities are located in the lodge.
What should I bring?
Most guests pack comfortable walking shoes and plan to drress for changing weather with light layers of clothing. It is also helpful to bring a flashlight and a sense of adventure!
